# Skills Matrix — Beginner → Working → Independent → Advanced

Use this for honest self-assessment quarterly. Rate yourself per row, date it, and keep
the history. The gap between your self-rating and your manager's rating is itself
information.

**Levels:**
- **B — Beginner:** knows the vocabulary, needs direction for every step.
- **W — Working:** executes defined tasks with review; knows when to ask.
- **I — Independent:** owns a scope; produces defensible deliverables with light review.
- **A — Advanced:** sets approach for others; handles ambiguity, novel facilities, and conflict.

---

## 1. Risk & Assessment

| Competency | B | W | I | A |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Security vocabulary | Recites definitions | Uses them correctly in writing | Uses them precisely in client meetings | Corrects misuse without condescension |
| Asset characterization | Lists obvious assets | Builds an asset register | Prioritizes by consequence with the owner | Elicits assets the owner hadn't articulated |
| Threat assessment | Knows threat categories | Applies a DBT given to them | Develops a defensible DBT from intelligence + history | Challenges an unrealistic DBT and reframes it |
| Vulnerability assessment | Follows a checklist | Runs a site survey solo | Identifies path-based vulnerabilities, not just missing devices | Models adversary paths and quantifies delay vs response |
| Risk analysis | Fills in a matrix | Justifies ratings | Explains matrix limitations and chooses method to fit decision | Advises on risk tolerance and residual risk acceptance |
| Security master planning | — | Contributes sections | Authors a phased plan with budget logic | Aligns security plan to business strategy and capital cycles |

## 2. Video Surveillance

| Competency | B | W | I | A |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Optics & imaging | Knows focal length affects zoom | Calculates FOV and DOF | Selects lens for scene geometry + light + target class | Diagnoses image problems from a still frame |
| Pixel density / DORI | Knows the four target classes | Calculates PPF for a given lens/distance | Sets per-zone targets tied to operational use | Defends a lower target where budget demands, with documented consequence |
| Camera selection | Names form factors | Matches form factor to application | Trades multisensor vs multiple fixed vs PTZ on cost/ports/licenses/maintenance | Predicts 7-year total cost of ownership implications |
| Coverage design | Places cameras where told | Covers entrances, chokepoints, corridors | Designs continuity of coverage and handoff between views | Designs to an investigative narrative, not a device count |
| Low light / IR / thermal | Knows IR exists | Understands IR range claims are optimistic | Designs lighting + camera as one system | Specifies thermal vs illuminated approaches by detection requirement |
| Encoding & bandwidth | Knows H.264/H.265 | Estimates bitrate per camera | Models aggregate + peak load with stated assumptions | Negotiates retention vs quality vs cost with the owner |
| Storage & retention | Knows retention in days | Calculates storage | Designs tiering, redundancy, failover, and growth headroom | Sizes for evidentiary and regulatory obligations |
| VMS architecture | Names components | Configures within a template | Designs server/storage/network topology and failover | Designs federated multi-site with WAN constraints |
